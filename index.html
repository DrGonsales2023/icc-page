Who This Is For

She knows exactly who she is.
She's just stopped acting like it.

ICC was built for the woman who has decades of leadership behind her — and a whole season of it still ahead. If any of these feel uncomfortably familiar, you're in the right place.

01
You've outgrown your current role — but haven't named what's next.
The title is fine. The work is fine. But "fine" stopped being enough a while ago, and you're not sure what to do with that.
02
AI feels like something happening to you, not something you use.
You're watching everyone else talk about AI fluency while quietly wondering if the ship has already sailed. It hasn't. But you need a different kind of on-ramp.
03
You're still proving yourself — and you're tired of it.
Decades of credibility, and you're still second-guessing your voice in the room. Something shifted. ICC is designed to help you find the shift — and reverse it.
04
Your wisdom is real. Your strategy for sharing it isn't.
You've built things, led people, solved problems. But translating that into a visible, relevant, influential presence? That's the work we do here.
05
You want a legacy — not just a résumé.
You're thinking about what you want to leave behind, not just what you've accumulated. That kind of clarity requires a different kind of leadership conversation.
06
You do your best thinking in good company.
Not a lecture. Not a webinar. A cohort of eight women who are equally serious, equally accomplished, and equally done with surface-level conversations.
This is not a program for beginners — and it's not for everyone.
ICC is for experienced women leaders, 50+, who have something real to build on. If you're looking for motivation, a quick fix, or someone to tell you what to do — this isn't it. If you're ready to do the thinking, trust your own compass, and lead from here — we'd love to meet you.
The Inner Compass Leadership Framework™

Five dimensions.
One direction — yours.

Developed from two decades of research on why brilliant people disengage — from classrooms, from careers, from themselves. Every dimension maps to a real disconnection. Every session reconnects one.

I
Dimension 01
Identity
Who you are at the core — not the title, not the role, not the version of yourself built for other people's expectations. The real one.
V
Dimension 02
Visibility
Being seen — fully, accurately, intentionally. Not performing. Not shrinking. Occupying your presence with authority and ease.
R
Dimension 03
Relevance
Staying current without losing yourself. Finding where your wisdom intersects with what the world — and AI — needs right now.
D
Dimension 04
Direction
Clarity about where you're going — and why. Not a five-year plan. A bearing. The kind that holds even when conditions change.
I
Dimension 05
Influence
The kind of leadership that extends beyond your title, your organization, your season. The impact that outlasts the role.
